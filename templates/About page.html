ABOUT PEER DEBUG

Learn Together. Debug Together.

Peer Debug is a collaborative platform designed to help students solve programming problems by connecting them with classmates, seniors, and teaching assistants.

💡 The Problem

Students often get stuck while coding and have to wait for a teacher or teaching assistant to become available. In a busy classroom, this can waste valuable learning time.

🚀 Our Solution

Peer Debug creates a simple help system where students can submit their coding doubts and other students, seniors, or TAs can pick them up and help solve them.

How Peer Debug Works

1

Ask for Help

Submit your coding problem, error, or doubt.

2

Find a Helper

A peer, senior, or TA can accept your request.

3

Debug Together

Discuss the problem and understand the solution.

4

Problem Solved

Mark the ticket as solved and continue learning.
