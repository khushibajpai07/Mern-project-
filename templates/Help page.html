Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is Peer Debug?

Peer Debug is a platform where students can ask programming-related doubts and get help from their peers, seniors, or teaching assistants.

❓ How do I ask for help?

Login to your account and select Ask for Help. Enter your problem, programming language, error, and code before submitting.

❓ Who can help me?

Other students, senior students, or teaching assistants can accept your help request.

❓ Can I help other students?

Yes. Login to Peer Debug and select Help a Peer to see open coding problems.

❓ What programming languages are supported?

The platform supports common languages such as C++, Python, JavaScript, Java, and more.

❓ What if my problem is not solved?

You can wait for another helper or provide additional information about your programming problem.

Still Need Help?

If you cannot find an answer, ask your teacher, TA, or Peer Debug administrator for assistance.
